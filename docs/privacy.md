# Privacy and safety

FitLoop can store sleep, mood, activity and focus information. Treat these records as sensitive even when the app is used for a university project.

- Collect only fields needed for the feature being tested.
- Keep real participant data out of source control, screenshots and test fixtures.
- Use pseudonymous accounts for an approved evaluation.
- Do not send account identifiers, email addresses or names to the coach service.
- Keep the coach service private; do not expose it directly to the public internet.
- Use HTTPS and secure cookie-based sessions before a production deployment.
- Add a clear account and data deletion process before recruiting participants.
- Explain retention periods and obtain the required ethics approval before collecting real participant data.

The coach gives habit prompts based on logged trends. It must not diagnose conditions, interpret mood as a mental-health assessment, prescribe treatment or create strict diet plans. Users should be able to ignore any suggestion.
